# MIRA — three-minute demo

Use a fresh browser profile at 390×844. The Site is private: sign in to your authorized account before presenting. Allow local storage. No API keys are needed.

## 0:00–0:35 — The morning decision

Open `/`.

Say: “MIRA answers one question: what do I wear today? This is a clearly labeled sample closet, with 42 pieces. The product opens at the decision, not at a dashboard.”

Tap “Why this?” and read the weather and occasion lines. Close the sheet. Change the occasion, then tap one alternative.

Say: “It gives reasons about the day and the clothes. It never grades the person. All of this works with a deterministic engine.”

## 0:35–1:05 — Wear and remember

Tap “Wear this.” Open Looks. Reopen the newest look.

Say: “One tap records the outfit. MIRA remembers what you reached for so the next recommendation can change. History keeps an illustrated record, even if an item leaves the wardrobe.”

## 1:05–1:40 — The actual wardrobe

Open Wardrobe. Filter Tops, then navy. Open a piece, change an attribute and save. Clear the filters. Open Add clothes, then choose two or three garment JPGs or PNGs. Review proposed tags, adjust a category and confirm.

Say: “Add a batch, correct the suggestions, confirm once. Photos stay on this device. The local fallback is honest about being a suggestion, so a model outage never blocks the closet.”

If optional background removal is unavailable, the original image remains usable after four seconds.

## 1:40–2:15 — A mirror, on a phone

Return to Mirror. Choose Upload a photo, or keep the silhouette. Tap “Swap a piece,” choose a different item, and view today’s look.

Say: “The mirror is a mode, not hardware. This is a two-dimensional layered preview. It makes the outfit tangible without pretending to be a generated fitting.”

Camera access is deferred in this release. Use a photo upload or the silhouette.

## 2:15–2:40 — Share deliberately

On `/today`, choose “Share this look” and create a link. Open it in a new tab signed in to the same authorized account.

Say: “Sharing sends only an illustrated snapshot, never my photo or my sizes. This deployment is private, so its links stay behind the account gate.”

## 2:40–3:00 — Ownership

Open Me. Show the language toggle, Export my data, and the shared-link revocation control. Open Delete everything, show the required typed confirmation, then cancel.

Say: “The wardrobe belongs to this browser. Export it, revoke a link, or erase it. MIRA’s job is to reduce a decision, not collect another version of you.”

Do not complete deletion before the demo is finished. “Start my wardrobe” switches to an empty personal closet and removes the seeded local history; use it only when ready to demonstrate a fresh capture flow.
